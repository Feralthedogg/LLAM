8. COMMERCIAL DEPLOYMENT AND MANDATORY DEFECT REPORTING

   8.1. Condition of Commercial Deployment.

        Compliance with this Section is a condition of the copyright and patent
        rights granted for Commercial Deployment. A material violation affects
        only those rights and only as provided in Section 15. It does not by
        itself affect rights exercised solely outside a Commercial Deployment.

   8.2. Reporting Duty.

        After obtaining Actual Knowledge of a Reportable Defect in connection
        with a Commercial Deployment, You must ensure that an initial report is
        Submitted in accordance with this Section:

      (a) for a Security Defect, without unreasonable delay and no later than
          seven calendar days after obtaining Actual Knowledge; and

      (b) for another Reportable Defect, no later than forty-five calendar days
          after obtaining Actual Knowledge.

        For a deadline stated in calendar days, the day on which Actual
        Knowledge arises is day zero, and the deadline expires at 23:59 UTC on
        the final day.

        An initial report may state that the issue is suspected, that the root
        cause is uncertain, or that investigation is ongoing. It does not need
        to prove that the Software is defective.

        If Actual Knowledge arises before Commercial Deployment begins, the
        reporting period begins on the first day of Commercial Deployment only
        when the suspected defect remains applicable to the Software then used
        and one or more members of the Responsible Personnel still possess the
        technical basis for the suspicion.

   8.3. No Duty to Discover.

        This License does not require You to conduct proactive testing, fuzzing,
        auditing, penetration testing, reverse engineering, monitoring,
        benchmarking, or code review; search for defects; reproduce a defect
        reported by another person; determine a definitive root cause; or
        continue investigating after a reasonable initial report.

        The reporting duty arises only from Actual Knowledge obtained during
        activities You otherwise choose to perform.

   8.4. Minimum Initial Report.

        To the extent reasonably known and available, the initial report must
        include:

      (a) the affected release, tag, commit, or package identifier;

      (b) the expected and observed behavior;

      (c) the known or reasonably suspected Material Technical Impact;

      (d) reproduction steps, logs, stack traces, crash data, a minimal
          reproducer, or other technical evidence when reasonably available;

      (e) relevant platform, architecture, compiler, backend, build mode, or
          Modification information when material to the suspected defect; and

      (f) a safe contact method for reasonable follow-up.

        If You later obtain material additional information through activities
        You otherwise perform, You must ensure that the material additional
        information is Submitted through an update to the existing report or an
        applicable Reporting Channel within forty-five calendar days after
        obtaining it, while the report remains open or is reasonably capable of
        receiving an update. You are not required to create new evidence or
        retain materials beyond Your ordinary retention practices solely because
        of this License.

   8.5. Security Reports.

        A Security Defect must first be reported through a private Reporting
        Channel. You must not publish exploit details, credentials,
        cryptographic secrets, personal data, customer data, or regulated data
        in a public issue merely to satisfy this License.

        If no private Reporting Channel is usable but the public issue tracker
        is usable, the deadline is satisfied when a public issue containing only
        the following information is Submitted through that public issue
        tracker:

      (a) the affected release or commit identifier;

      (b) a statement that a private security contact is requested; and

      (c) a safe contact method.

        Technical details must then be Submitted through the usable private
        channel within seven calendar days after the Licensor communicates that
        channel to You through the contact method supplied in the fallback
        notice.

   8.6. Confidential, Privileged, Personal, and Restricted Information.

        This License does not require You to disclose:

      (a) the complete source code of a Separate Work;

      (b) trade secrets unrelated to the defect;

      (c) credentials, cryptographic secrets, personal data, customer data, or

      (d) attorney-client communications, attorney work product, or other

   8.7. Duplicate Reports.

        No duplicate report is required when the same defect affecting the same
        relevant releases is known to You to be publicly tracked or has been

   8.8. Unavailable or Unusable Reporting Channel.

        A Reporting Channel is unusable when submission is technically or
        legally impossible, including because the Official Repository or channel

        a reasonable internal record under Your ordinary security and retention
        practices. You must ensure that the report is Submitted within seven
        calendar days after a usable method becomes available.
